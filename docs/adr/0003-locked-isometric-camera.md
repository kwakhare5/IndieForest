# 0003 — Locked Isometric Framing with Subtle Cursor Parallax

We lock camera orbit rotation and panning, fixing the viewport to the classic isometric perspective `[18, 18, 18]` at `zoom={44}` with subtle cursor parallax.

Free-rotation controls turn widgets into disorienting 3D sandboxes. A locked framing gives a stable, predictable diorama feel while retaining clamped zoom and clickable tree inspection.
