# 0004 — Warm Studio Linen Off-White Aesthetic

We use a soft off-white alabaster/linen background (`#ece7de` to `#e2ded4`) and warm stone contact shadows instead of stark pure white or dark mode.

Pure white `#ffffff` causes severe visual glare, while dark mode hides low-poly geometry details. Warm Studio Linen gives an organic papercraft diorama feel that is comfortable for all-day use.
